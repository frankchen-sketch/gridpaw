Privacy Policy

Short version: Pictomino collects no personal information from kids. No accounts, no logins, no forms. Just puzzles and fun.

1. Play Anonymously — Or Sign In Optionally

Pictomino is a free puzzle game that is fully playable without any account. All game progress (stars, level) is stored locally in your browser using localStorage and never leaves your device unless you choose to sign in.

1a. Optional Google Sign-In

Older players may optionally sign in with Google to sync their daily-challenge streak across devices. Signing in is entirely optional and intended for users 13 and older. If you sign in, we collect and store only what Google shares after you approve:

  • Your Google account name and profile picture
  • Your Google account email (used only to identify your account; never shown publicly)
  • Your Google account ID (a random number Google uses to identify you to websites)
  • Your daily challenge progress (streak, best streak, best time)

We use this data for exactly one purpose: syncing your puzzle progress and showing the daily leaderboard. We do not send marketing emails, sell or share your data, or post anything to your Google account. Sign-in uses a secure first-party session cookie (pt_session) containing no personal information. You can sign out at any time. To delete your account and all stored progress, contact us below.

2. Anonymous Traffic Statistics (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to count visits and understand which pages are helpful — for example, how many visitors open the game and how long they play. GA4 runs in anonymous aggregate mode: it does not identify individual users, and we never combine it with any personal information. We do not track individual players across the web.

3. Advertising

Pictomino currently shows no ads at all. If we ever add advertising, it will be context-based (matched to the page content, never to a child's behavior) and fully compliant with child-directed content rules. We will never run behavioral or personalized ads aimed at children.

4. Third-Party Services

The third-party services on this site are Google Analytics (anonymous traffic statistics) and, for players who choose to sign in, Google Sign-In. Google's handling of account data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. We do not use tracking cookies, fingerprinting, or advertising cookies of any kind.

5. Parents' Rights & Contact

We believe in the strictest privacy standard for children's products. Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing to delete — but if you have any question about privacy on Pictomino, or if you believe any data was collected unintentionally, please email us and we will respond promptly and remove anything you ask us to remove.
