**Subject:** GridPaw: telling you before, not after

Hi Peter,

Back in October you pulled GridPaw from DLES.gg because our ads were redirecting players to spam sites. You also said the part that bothered you was not trusting that we wouldn't quietly put the same ads back. So this is the "before".

We are bringing advertising back, in one form only: fixed-size display banners on our guide and reference pages. No native cards (those were behind the October complaints), no pop-unders, no social bar, no smartlink, nothing that opens a new tab or moves a visitor off the page. Every slot carries an Advertisement label, no ad sits inside a puzzle or over a game control, and the game pages and the home board stay clean.

The commitment is published rather than promised. It is on our changelog, where anyone can check it at any time: https://gridpaw.com/changelog/ — it says a single redirect report takes the whole network down the same day, and that entry stays on the page.

I am asking, not announcing. If you would rather GridPaw ran no ads at all, say so and the banners stay off. If you are fine with static banners under that tripwire, I will switch them on after this exchange and drop you a line when it is done. Either way, if a player ever reports a redirect you can delist us permanently without asking me again, and you will not have to argue with me about it.

Thanks for running DLES.gg, and for calling the redirect out in the first place instead of just quietly dropping us.

Frank
GridPaw — https://gridpaw.com
